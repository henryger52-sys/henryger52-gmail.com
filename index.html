Digital Security

We secure every port your cargo passes through.

The ones ships dock at, and the ones your data moves through.

Modern freight runs on connected systems — booking platforms, EDI feeds, customs APIs, tracking dashboards. We treat that infrastructure with the same discipline as a physical terminal: least-privilege access, continuous monitoring, and encryption by default.

  • TLS 1.3
    Encrypted document exchange

    Bills of lading, invoices, and customs paperwork move over encrypted channels only, never plain email attachments.

  • RBAC
    Access-controlled tracking

    Shipment visibility is scoped per account — you see your cargo, and only your cargo.

  • 24/7
    Monitored infrastructure

    Booking and tracking systems are watched around the clock, with alerting tuned to unusual account activity.

  • VERIFIED
    Fraud-aware operations

    Every payment or booking-detail change is verified through a second channel — a standard defense against freight-invoice fraud.
