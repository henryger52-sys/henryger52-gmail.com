The Shipper's Cargo & Data Security Checklist

18 checks our operations team runs before every FCL and LCL booking — documentation, container prep, and cargo data security in one list.

Request a Quote

1. Documentation & Customs Compliance

  • Commercial invoice matches the purchase order value and currency exactly.
  • Packing list itemizes weight, volume, and carton count per line item.
  • Bill of lading details are cross-checked against the booking confirmation before cutoff.
  • HS codes are verified for the destination country, not assumed from origin classification.
  • Certificates of origin and any required permits are confirmed with the destination customs broker.
  • Insurance coverage is confirmed for the full cargo value, not just the carrier's default liability.

2. Container & Cargo Preparation

  • Container inspection for structural damage, odor, and moisture before loading (the "7-point check").
  • Weight distribution is balanced to avoid exceeding axle limits at destination.
  • VGM (Verified Gross Mass) is submitted within the carrier's cutoff window.
  • Seals are ISO 17712-compliant and the seal number is logged against the booking.
  • Reefer settings (temperature, ventilation, humidity) are confirmed in writing for temperature-controlled cargo.
  • Photos of the loaded container and seal are kept on file before gate-in.

3. Data & Cargo Security

  • Document exchange happens over encrypted channels — never shipping instructions as plain email attachments.
  • Bank and payment details for any freight partner are verified by phone before a first payment, every time they change.
  • Booking access is limited to the people who actually need it, with individual logins — not one shared account.
  • Tracking credentials are rotated when an employee or partner offboards.
  • Suspicious booking-change requests (urgent, off-hours, unfamiliar sender) are confirmed through a known contact channel before acting.
  • Incident contact at your carrier/forwarder is on file in case of a suspected data or account compromise.
